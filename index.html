Welcome to the Akan Name Generator

The **Akan people** are one of the largest ethnic groups in **Ghana** and **Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast)**.
They are known for their rich cultural heritage, including traditional festivals, colorful kente cloth, skilled gold craftsmanship, and the Akan language.
The Akan have a matrilineal system of inheritance, where family lineage and inheritance are traced through the mother's side.
They have played an important role in the history, culture, and economy of West Africa.
They had a very unique way of naming where they used the days of the week.
